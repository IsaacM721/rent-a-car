MotoresRD — First Decision: Platform

This choice shapes the entire tech stack. In the Dominican Republic, Android holds ~78% of the smartphone market.

A

iOS Only (Swift / SwiftUI)

Ship fast. Match your existing codebase. Build the rider + driver apps in SwiftUI like you already know. Get to market first, then expand later.

⚠ Reaches ~22% of DR smartphone users

B

Cross-Platform (React Native)

One codebase, both iOS and Android. Widely used for Uber-style apps (Lyft, Shopify use it). JavaScript/TypeScript — different from Swift but massive community.

✓ Reaches 100% of DR smartphone users

C

Cross-Platform (Flutter)

Google's framework. Dart language. Beautiful native-feeling UIs on both platforms. Growing fast and well-suited for ride-hailing apps.

✓ Reaches 100% of DR smartphone users

My recommendation: B — React Native. The DR market is Android-heavy and ride-hailing is won on driver density, which means you need as many drivers as possible on day one. React Native also has battle-tested libraries specifically for ride-hailing (maps, sockets, payments).
